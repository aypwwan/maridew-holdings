About

Rooted in Kenya. Active across Africa.

Maridew Holdings Ltd is a multi-disciplinary land, infrastructure and built-environment company delivering integrated solutions from raw land to completed, managed assets.

  • Land identification, due diligence and title advisory
  • Surveying, geospatial and geotechnical services
  • Planning, design and development approvals
  • Civil, structural and MEP engineering
  • Construction supervision and quality delivery
  • Real estate development and asset management

Our Vision

To be Kenya's most trusted partner in the built environment — a firm whose land and infrastructure work changes the shape of the communities we serve.

Our Mission

To integrate land, engineering and property services under one accountable brand, delivering credible, sustainable outcomes for clients, institutions and investors across Kenya and the region.
